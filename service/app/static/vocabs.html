Cost & mechanics

The gap between buy and sell price, in points of $0.01. It explodes at rollover, Sunday open, and news — a scalp targeting less than ~3× spread donates money.
entries refused above 500 points
1 lot = 100 oz of gold. So a $1.00 price move is $100 per lot, or $1 on 0.01 lots. A $45 stop on 0.05 lots risks $225 — do this math before entering, not after.
Gold has no real "pips" — it moves in points of $0.01. When someone says "gold moved 500 pips" they usually mean $5.00. Think in dollars to stay safe.
At EU 1:20 leverage and $4,500 gold, even 0.01 lots needs ~$225 of margin. Pyramiding multiplies the requirement — three stacked positions need triple.
The nightly financing charge for holding a position. Long gold usually pays negative swap, tripled Wednesday to cover the weekend. Irrelevant intraday.
flatten-before-break closes everything before the daily break
A stop is a request, not a guarantee — in fast tape it fills at the next available price, which around news can be dollars away. This is why backtests model extra cost per trade.
Brokers enforce a minimum distance between price and your stop, and freeze changes when price gets close. A very tight stop can simply be rejected by the server.
ECN accounts charge per lot (~$6–7 round trip) in exchange for tighter spreads. Cost per trade is the number a high-frequency style multiplies hardest.

Volatility & range

The average size of recent bars — the market's current "breathing depth". Stops sized in ATR multiples adapt when volatility changes; fixed-dollar stops don't.
stops = 2.0× ATR default, 1.75× on M15
How far gold travels in a typical day — lately $50–100+. Once today's range is spent, chasing continuation is low-odds, and targets beyond the remaining range are wishes.
Which "personality" the market is in. The same signal behaves differently in each — chop is where trend systems bleed.
classified via ADX + ATR percentile on every signal
Gold reopens Sunday wherever weekend news left it. Stops do not protect through a gap — they fill at the open, however far away that is.
Big days follow big days. After a $100 shock day, expect another wide one — widen stops and expectations instead of assuming calm returns.

Drivers

Gold is priced in dollars, so dollar strength is a headwind for every long. Check DXY's direction before fighting a gold trend.
The 10-year TIPS yield is gold's big-picture driver: gold pays no interest, so rising real yields make holding it more expensive. Multi-month moves track this.
Gold's violence hours — spreads triple and stops slip dollars. A blocked entry near news is protection working, not a bug.
NewsGuard blocks ±30 min around high-impact USD events
Shocks bid gold — but in a true liquidation panic gold falls with everything, sold to raise margin. The haven bid returns after the deleveraging.
Central-bank buying has been the structural bid of this bull market. ETF holdings and COT positioning show what big money is doing — slow data that frames the trend you trade inside.
Silver is high-beta gold: when silver leads a move, it often signals conviction. A stretched gold/silver ratio flags regime turns.

Structure & execution

Gold respects the $50 grid ($4,450, $4,500…). Stops cluster on these levels and get hunted — park yours beyond the level plus a margin, never exactly on it.
The most-watched intraday levels. A sweep below yesterday's low that instantly reclaims is a classic long trigger; acceptance beyond it is a different trade.
An engineered wick through an obvious level to harvest the stops parked there, then reverse. If your stop sits where everyone's does, you are the liquidity.
Size from the stop, not from conviction: lots = (equity × risk%) ÷ (stop distance × $100). Doubling after losses is how gold accounts die.

(win% × avg win) − (loss% × avg loss), after costs. Win rate alone means nothing — this system's M15 lane wins ~41% and still profits because losers are smaller than winners.
Losses compound against you: −20% needs +25% to recover, −50% needs +100%. Circuit breakers exist because of this asymmetry.
