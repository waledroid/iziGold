# S/R zone wall sweep — increment 2 of the S/R zones spec (2026-09-06)

Question: should an entry that sits inside, or just below/above, an opposing
support/resistance box be refused? (spec
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-05-sr-zones-design.md`; the live EA logs the
same verdict report-only as `sr_agree`/`sr_tier`.)

Method: `scripts/backtest.py --sr-zone-tiers TIERS --sr-zone-room-atr K` on
`bars_max.json` (M5, 2025-03-19 → 2026-08-21, 101,084 bars), $10,000 start,
halves split at 2025-12-04 (calendar midpoint). The replay rebuilds the three
tiers (H4/H1/M15) at every signal decision from tier bars CLOSED by that bar,
with the identical engine the EA mirrors (`app.sr_zones`). Refusals were
spot-checked (40/40) against a from-scratch full-history rebuild; no zone is
born after its decision bar. Recipes: M15 lane = the live rider (FIXED 0.05
lots, confirm 1, 1.75 ATR stop, EMA-50, 0.3 ATR clearance; baseline +$9,974.78
reproduces the 2026-09-01 study to the cent). M5 lane = replay defaults (the
live EA inputs via `config/strategy.json`), risk-sized 1%; the fixed-lot
re-run below removes compounding.

House standard: a filter passes only if BOTH halves are at least as good as
baseline on net, and drawdown is not worse.

## Verdict

- **M5 lane: PASS — refuse entries inside / within 0.5 ATR of an M15-tier
  box.** Fixed-lot: net +58% (+$5,641 → +$8,920), max dd −28%, both halves
  better, on a 0.25–0.75 plateau (every step passes). Risk-sized: +154% /
  dd −29.5% (compounding inflates the %, same shape). Trades −27%.
- **M15 lane: FAIL everywhere.** On the trend-rider, "wall" entries are its
  BEST trades (H1-wall +$4,572 at 50% wins vs "clear" +$772 at 36%): riding
  through levels is the edge. Never gate this lane on S/R.
- H4 and H1 tiers add nothing on M5 (H4-wall entries are its best group;
  H1-wall about flat). The signal is the M15 swing box: buying right under a
  recent M15 swing high (or selling right above a swing low) on M5 is the
  failed-breakout entry the lane bleeds on.
